# Support Policy and SLA

_Last updated: 2026-02-10_

This document describes standard AtlasFlow support availability and target response times.

## Support channels

AtlasFlow provides support through the customer support portal and support email channels appropriate to the subscription level.

## Severity levels

AtlasFlow commonly uses the following severity labels when triaging support issues:

- **Sev1:** critical service disruption or business-critical blockage
- **Sev2:** major impairment with meaningful operational impact
- **Sev3:** standard issue or degraded behavior with workaround available
- **Sev4:** question, minor defect, or general guidance request

## Plan-based support commitments

### Starter
Support availability:

- standard support during business hours

Target first response:

- within **2 business days**

### Growth
Support availability:

- standard support during business hours

Target first response:

- within **8 business hours**

### Enterprise
Support availability:

- priority support options
- **24/5 coverage for Sev1 issues**
- business-hours handling for non-Sev1 issues unless otherwise contracted

Additional notes:

- a dedicated Customer Success Manager may be included or purchased depending on contract
- contract-specific terms may override standard defaults

## Business hours

Unless otherwise stated by contract, business-hours support is handled Monday to Friday excluding standard regional support holidays for the serving support team.

## Scope notes

Response targets are first-response goals, not guaranteed resolution times.

Time to resolution depends on factors such as:

- issue severity
- reproducibility
- customer responsiveness
- whether third-party dependencies are involved
- whether the issue is caused by workspace configuration rather than platform behavior

## Best practice for faster support

Customers should provide:

- workspace identifier
- affected workflow or project
- time window of the issue
- relevant error text or screenshots
- example user identifiers where relevant
