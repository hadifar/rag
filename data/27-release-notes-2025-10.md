# Release Notes – October 2025

_Release window: 2025-10-07_

This release introduced several commercial and platform updates.

## Highlights

### New Billing Admin role
We introduced the **Billing Admin** role for organizations that want finance users to manage invoices and payment methods without giving them full workspace administration rights.

### API limit update for Growth
The default **Growth** API rate limit was increased from the previous standard to **120 requests per minute**.

### Slack approval actions remain Enterprise-only
At the time of this release, interactive approval actions in Slack were available only on **Enterprise**. Starter and Growth continued to support Slack notifications without approval actions.

## Notes

This document reflects platform behavior at the time of the October 2025 release. Later product updates may supersede specific limits or feature availability described here.
