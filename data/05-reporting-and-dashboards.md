# Reporting and Dashboards

AtlasFlow includes dashboards and export tools for monitoring operational activity across projects and workflows.

## Dashboard types

Common dashboard views include:

- workflow completion summaries
- overdue task views
- approval bottleneck views
- exception and failure views
- project-level operational summaries

Dashboards can be filtered by project, workflow, owner, time window, status, and other available fields depending on the workspace plan and enabled modules.

## Viewing dashboards

Users can view dashboards when they have access to the relevant project or dashboard configuration.

Typical read access may be granted to:

- Workspace Owners
- Admins
- Members with project access
- Viewers with dashboard access

## Export formats

Supported export formats include:

- CSV
- XLSX
- PDF

The exact fields exported depend on the dashboard and filters used at the time of export.

## Manual exports

Manual exports are available on all plans to users who have permission to view the underlying dashboard and are allowed to export from that view.

Manual exports are commonly used for stakeholder updates, monthly reviews, and offline analysis.

## Scheduled exports

Scheduled report exports are available on **Growth** and **Enterprise** plans only.

Typical schedule options include:

- daily
- weekly
- monthly

Scheduled exports can be delivered by email to approved recipients or stored according to the configured export destination behavior available in the workspace.

## Common limitations

- scheduled exports are not available on Starter
- very large exports may take longer to generate
- users need access to the underlying dashboard or report definition
- export quality depends on the filters and date window selected at the time the schedule is created
