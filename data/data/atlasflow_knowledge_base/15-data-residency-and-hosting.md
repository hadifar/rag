# Data Residency and Hosting

_Last updated: 2026-02-01_

This document summarizes standard AtlasFlow hosting behavior and regional data residency options.

## Hosting model

AtlasFlow is delivered as a hosted SaaS service.

AtlasFlow does **not** support customer-managed on-prem deployment.

## Standard regions

AtlasFlow supports hosted service operation in the following standard regional footprints:

- European Union region
- United States region

The exact infrastructure footprint may evolve over time, but regional service commitments are defined at the contract and plan level rather than by a public list of individual data center locations.

## Regional availability by plan

### Starter
Starter workspaces use AtlasFlow’s standard hosted model and may not include customer-specific regional guarantees.

### Growth
Growth workspaces may select from available regional offerings where supported during workspace creation or contract setup.

### Enterprise
Enterprise customers may receive contractual regional storage and processing commitments, subject to product availability and the signed agreement.

## What data residency means in AtlasFlow

In AtlasFlow, data residency refers to the regional handling of customer workspace data according to the product and contract terms in force.

It does not imply that every connected third-party integration stores or processes data in the same region. Customers should evaluate third-party integration behavior separately.

## Notes

This page covers hosting and regional residency at a policy level. It does not list every regulatory framework or customer-specific contractual term.
