# AtlasFlow Overview

AtlasFlow is a B2B SaaS platform for teams that manage recurring operational work across departments.

It is designed for organizations that need a structured way to coordinate repeatable work such as approvals, monthly checks, onboarding steps, finance reviews, internal compliance tasks, or integration-driven handoffs between teams. Typical customers include operations teams, HR teams, finance and administration teams, and internal tooling or IT teams in mid-size companies.

AtlasFlow combines several capabilities in one product:

- workflow automation
- task assignment and tracking
- approval routing
- reporting and dashboards
- integrations with tools such as Slack, Jira, Google Drive, and email

## How AtlasFlow is organized

The product is organized around a few core concepts:

### Workspaces
A workspace is the top-level container for a company or business unit. Workspace settings control things such as branding, time zone, authentication options, integrations, and notification defaults.

### Projects
Projects group related work. Teams often create projects by department, region, or business process. Workflows, dashboards, and access settings can be managed at the project level.

### Workflows
A workflow defines a repeatable process. Workflows can contain steps, conditions, approvals, due dates, and integration actions. Workflows can be run manually or triggered automatically.

### Automations
Automations connect AtlasFlow events to actions, reminders, or integrations. Examples include sending a Slack notification when an approval is overdue or creating a Jira issue when a workflow step fails.

### Dashboards and reports
Dashboards summarize operational activity across projects and workflows. Teams use them to monitor completion rates, overdue work, approval bottlenecks, and exceptions. Reports can be exported for internal review or stakeholder updates.

### Integrations and API
AtlasFlow can be used through the web application and through API-based integrations. Customers often connect AtlasFlow to Slack, Jira, email, and internal systems.

## Typical use cases

AtlasFlow is commonly used for:

- recurring operations checklists
- onboarding and offboarding processes
- finance review workflows
- internal audit preparation
- approvals across multiple stakeholders
- issue escalation and follow-up

## Product access

AtlasFlow is available as a hosted web application. Many customers use the web app for day-to-day work and add API access or integrations where they need automation or custom system-to-system workflows.

## What AtlasFlow is not

AtlasFlow is not positioned as a general document management platform, a full ERP, or a general-purpose chat assistant. It is focused on structured, repeatable work and the reporting and automation around that work.
