# Billing FAQ

## Who can view invoices?
Invoices can typically be viewed by:

- Workspace Owner
- Billing Admin

Admins without billing privileges do not automatically get invoice access.

## Who can update payment methods?
Payment methods can typically be updated by:

- Workspace Owner
- Billing Admin

This is one of the main reasons organizations assign the Billing Admin role to finance users.

## What happens when we add seats mid-cycle?
Seat increases may be prorated depending on the billing arrangement in effect.

The exact line items depend on whether the subscription is monthly or annual and whether the change occurs during an active billing period.

## When does cancellation take effect?
Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current paid term or billing period, not immediately.

For annual subscriptions, cancellation usually stops renewal for the next term while service continues for the remainder of the prepaid period.

## Are annual subscriptions refundable?
Annual subscriptions are generally non-refundable after the first 30 days unless required by law or otherwise stated in a contract.

## Why is a former user still receiving invoice emails?
A person may still receive billing-related email if they remain configured as a billing contact, even if their day-to-day workspace access has been removed.

To stop those messages:

- update the billing contact in billing settings
- verify that the user is no longer listed for invoice or renewal notifications

## VAT / tax notes
Tax treatment depends on the billing country, the customer profile, and the information on file. Customers should ensure billing details remain current to avoid invoice issues.
