# Refund and Cancellation Policy

_Last updated: 2026-01-12_

This document summarizes the standard billing rules for cancellation, downgrade timing, and refunds.

## Subscription terms

AtlasFlow subscriptions are generally sold as:

- monthly subscriptions
- annual subscriptions

The commercial agreement or order form governs where contract-specific terms differ from this policy.

## Monthly subscriptions

For monthly subscriptions:

- cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- access continues until the end of the paid period unless otherwise stated
- future charges stop after the cancellation becomes effective

## Annual subscriptions

For annual subscriptions:

- cancellation stops automatic renewal for the next annual term
- access continues for the remainder of the prepaid term unless otherwise stated by contract

### Refund rule for annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are generally **non-refundable after the first 30 days**, unless a refund is required by law or explicitly provided for in a contract.

## Downgrades

Plan downgrades generally take effect at the next renewal point rather than immediately.

This is especially important when a downgrade would remove capabilities currently in use, such as:

- SSO
- scheduled exports
- workspace API keys
- webhooks

## Trials

Where a trial is offered:

- the trial converts according to the terms presented at signup or in the order process
- the customer is responsible for canceling before conversion if they do not want the subscription to begin

## Billing changes during active terms

Seat increases or other commercial changes may be prorated according to the billing arrangement in effect.

## Notes

This document is a policy summary. The order form, contract, or legally required local rules prevail where different.
